unchanged, checksum-verified original UPCT orange observations. They are
publication assets for the independent manuscript *The Best Orange Coating
Depends on What You Measure*; they are **not** synthetic illustrations.

The [initial R workflow run](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/ds-projects-portfolio/actions/runs/42370138665)
completed successfully and produced these exact files with the two descriptive
CSV reports and endpoint contrasts. The archived artifact had SHA-256
`69e956f954a4a4495336e97e5c8c67e54d00a5128045eaf7be8983feef8ea674`.

Regenerate using:

```bash
cd projects/citrus_postharvest_empirical
Rscript tests/test_empirical_sources.R
Rscript scripts/run.R data/raw results/generated
```

Charts connect treatment-by-day **group summaries**, not fruit-level
trajectories. The publication must distinguish the separate inoculated-decay,
weight-loss and firmness panels. For data provenance, refer to
[data/raw/PROVENANCE.md](data/raw/PROVENANCE.md) and [METHODS.md](METHODS.md).
