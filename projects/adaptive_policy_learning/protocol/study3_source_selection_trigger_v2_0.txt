Adaptive Policy Learning Study 3 source-selection trigger v2.0

Purpose: after merge to main, execute the prospectively frozen Study 3 source-selection audit on the official locked Open Bandit Dataset archive.

Candidate campaigns: men, women.

Scientific boundary: this workflow may inspect provenance, schema, row counts, timestamps, item IDs, position labels, propensity support, and item-context catalogs. Selection rule is frozen in study3_source_selection_lock_v2_0.json. The selected campaign must be frozen before a separate Study 3 empirical design is specified and before any selected-campaign click outcome is parsed.
